**Study:** Does Load Bear make household admin lighter?
**Researchers:** [Name, organisation, email, phone]
**Ethics approval:** [Committee name and approval number]

## What is this study about?
We want to find out whether using Load Bear for six weeks makes keeping on top of household admin feel lighter
and take less time.

## What would I do?
- Use Load Bear as you normally would for six weeks.
- Answer a starting survey (about 4 minutes), a one-minute check-in each week, and a final survey (about 6 minutes)
  in the app. You can skip any question.
- Optionally, a 20-minute conversation at the end (we'll ask separately).

## What information is collected?
- Your survey answers (numbers only).
- Counts of how you used Load Bear: for example how many things you gave Bear, how many reminders you got, how many
  things were done, and how many corrections you made.
- We **do not** collect what your items say, your documents, names, children's details, amounts, or anything you type.
- Your data is labelled with a code, not your name or email. Only [Researcher] holds the key that links the code to
  the app account, and it is kept separate from the data.

## Who will see it, and where is it stored?
Load Bear stores data with Supabase and Vercel [confirm regions]. If photo/PDF reading is switched on, images you give
Bear are processed by Anthropic. The research team receives only the coded export. Results will be reported in
summary form; no individual will be identifiable.

## Do I have to take part?
No. Taking part is voluntary and won't affect your use of Load Bear. You can leave at any time in Settings → Research
study → Leave the study, and choose to delete your answers. Data already included in published summaries can't be removed.

## Risks and benefits
The main cost is your time. Thinking about your workload might be uncomfortable; you can skip any question. There may
be no direct benefit, but you'll help us learn whether apps like this genuinely help.

## How long is data kept?
[e.g. 5 years after publication, as required by the ethics committee], then securely deleted.

## Questions or complaints
Contact [Researcher]. If you have concerns about how the study is run, contact [Ethics committee contact].
