# Ethics and approvals (not legal advice)

- Research with people in Australia should be reviewed against the *National Statement on Ethical Conduct in Human
  Research* (NHMRC, 2023). A low-risk pilot may qualify for low-risk review, but that decision belongs to a Human
  Research Ethics Committee (HREC), not to the researchers.
- Options: a university HREC (if partnering with an academic), or an independent HREC that reviews commercial
  research. Budget time (often weeks) and any fees.
- Before recruiting: name the researchers and contacts in the information sheet; confirm data regions and
  subprocessors (APP 8); decide retention; prepare a data management plan; decide whether exit interviews are
  recorded (separate consent).
- Conflicts of interest: the founder owns Load Bear; disclose this. Ideally an independent researcher analyses the data.
- If results will be used commercially (e.g. in acquisition talks), say so in the information sheet.
