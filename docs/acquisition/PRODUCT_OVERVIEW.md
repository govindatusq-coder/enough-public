# Product overview

**Load Bear: Life Admin, Handled.** Consumer brand: "Mama Bear's got you."

People give Bear anything on their mind (a bill, a school notice, a to-do, a messy list) by typing, voice,
photo, PDF, the iPhone share sheet, or (when enabled) by forwarding an email. Bear works out what each
responsibility needs, does the parts it genuinely can (keeping deadlines, preparing details and drafts,
planning reminders, grouping repeat notices), and asks the person only for the smallest action that truly
needs them. It says nothing when nothing needs them.

**What it does today (implemented and tested):** evidence-aware extraction (facts vs inferences), ten
responsibility patterns with dependency plans, a full School Admin pack, duplicate and update handling,
priority and calm intervention, an honest lifecycle (only "verified" with evidence), My Wins from real events,
an enterprise API with tenant isolation (off by default), and a forward-to-Bear email pipeline (off by default).

**What it does not do:** read inboxes or calendars, see bank accounts, pay, submit forms, book, or contact
anyone. Every such capability is reported as NOT_SUPPORTED.

**Enterprise thesis (hypothesis, not evidence):** an optional employee benefit inside an HR/benefits platform
(Employment Hero is the reference case). No partner, pilot, customer or revenue exists today.

**Evidence status:** a synthetic benchmark (213 scenarios) and 500+ automated tests. No user study yet.
