# Commercial assumptions (HYPOTHETICAL)

The editable model is `commercial/assumptions.json`; run `node commercial/model.mjs` to regenerate
`commercial/scenarios.md`. With the current placeholder assumptions:

- Conservative (20,000 covered, A$1/employee/month): ~A$0.24M annual revenue.
- Base (50,000 covered, A$2): ~A$1.2M annual revenue, ~A$1.0M contribution.
- Optimistic (150,000 covered, A$2.50): ~A$4.5M annual revenue.

These depend almost entirely on the platform paying per covered employee, which is unvalidated. They are
not forecasts and not Employment Hero figures. No customer, pilot or letter of intent exists. A credible
valuation also needs demand evidence, a pilot, legal review and due diligence.
