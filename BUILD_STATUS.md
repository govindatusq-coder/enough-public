# Load Bear — BUILD STATUS

Last updated: 25 October 2026.

## Completed
- Brand board studied; visual system documented (`docs/load-bear/00-visual-system.md`).
- Architecture, data model, extraction schema, vertical slice, milestones and external configuration documented (`docs/load-bear/01`–`06`).
- **Sharing to Bear, step 1 (26 Oct):** the capture box has a "Paste from clipboard" button (for text copied in Messenger, WhatsApp, Messages or anything else), and the web app manifest declares a share target at /bear/share, so Android can share text and links straight into the capture box. iPhone web apps cannot appear in the share sheet; the iPhone route is a native Share Extension in the TestFlight app (step 2, needs Xcode on a Mac, not yet built or tested). Messenger and WhatsApp expose less than Messages through the share sheet, so copy-and-paste and screenshots stay the dependable routes. 202 tests.
- **Self-updating app (26 Oct):** when Bear comes back to the front on an older build it now reloads itself once per version (unless something is being typed or a sheet is open, then the banner waits), and a copy opened from an old deployment or preview link shows "This is an old link to Load Bear" with a button to the live address. A phone showing an old version is almost always a home-screen icon or tab saved from an older link: open the live address once and it keeps itself current from then on.
- **My Wins tiles and colour coding (26 Oct):** the four tiles (things handled, money protected, time protected, things for me) have artwork and open a sheet showing exactly what is behind each number: handled things, each saving with its reason and whether you told Bear, time that was actually measured, and things for you. Empty ones say honestly why. Completed cards are colour coded by area with a small label. The "Bear's got the rest" line is removed from My Wins. 200 tests.
- **Low-pressure tasks, overdue alerts, handling banner (26 Oct):** Bear reads every task two ways and shows its reading on one compact card with one-tap changes: time-sensitive or no rush, and one go or bigger. Only time-sensitive tasks get planned reminder times; "no rush" tasks never get a date or time unless the person picks "now and then" (every week, fortnight or month, with gentle wording and no escalation) or a day of their own, or chooses no reminders. Bigger tasks are broken into small steps by the AI service. Capture now has a type-straight-away box (no menu step), bills are planned automatically once checked (no plan sheet), tasks need no verify screen, and onboarding can be skipped at any step. Anything time-sensitive that is past its date is flagged clearly at the top of My Bear, in Needs you, in its area, on its own page and on the check screen when it is uploaded already late; "no rush" items can never be overdue. Bear's handling is now a banner with three numbers (bills watched, things held, reminders set), bear on the left, placed last with space above the nav. Area tiles use full-width artwork. 198 tests.
- **Separate purchases and a wishlist (25 Oct):** splitting a list now makes real separate tasks (2 to 6, each with its own project, items, cost, date and reminder, shown as "Part 2 of 5"; the original is archived and each purchase is counted in My Wins on its own). For a spend of $200 or more Bear asks once when it will be comfortable to buy: now, on a date, or not sure, in which case it offers to check back in 2 weeks, a month or 3 months, or just wishlist it. Anything but "now" moves the item to a Wishlist that lives as one small tile inside Your areas (only when it has something), stays off the busy screens, and comes back as one reminder on the check-back day. Prices can be typed after an item in the edit box ("drill bits $25"). Tasks Bear sets up no longer pop open the reminder sheet by themselves; a small "Set a reminder" button is there instead. 187 tests.
- **Changing banner (25 Oct):** the My Bear banner is now a short greeting plus one line, with the picture fixed to its own right-hand column so it can never be squeezed. The picture and line change on every visit, chosen from the artwork library for the time of day and the day mode (Wiped and Chaos days only get gentle lines), never repeating the last one. The name now comes from the login (sign-up name or Google profile) and is saved automatically; the banner no longer asks for it (Settings can still change it). 182 tests.
- **My Bear banner, phone clock, visible costs (25 Oct):** My Bear opens with a greeting banner (name, bigger, full mama bear artwork); the "Not yet" and Health/Wealth/Happiness sections are removed; area tiles use the supplied area artwork; Bear asks once for a name when it has none (also editable in Settings). Bear now follows the phone: the device time zone is sent once per session, saved, and any waiting reminder is planned again for local time (previously everything assumed Sydney); times on screen use the phone clock; reminder text uses readable dates. Estimated costs show at the top of a list task, lists are priced automatically at set-up when the AI service is connected, and area rows show the estimate. 180 tests, tsc, scoped lint, build and a 27-step walk-through pass.
- **Areas, Settings and editing wins (25 Oct):** My Bear has a "Your areas" section; tap an area to see what is current in it (and what is done), including steps and purchases under their main task. The Me tab is now Settings with a cog icon. In My Wins each win has Edit: tell Bear about a saving (counted and labelled "you told Bear", replaceable or removable), open the details, or make it current again (works for bills too, and removes the win and its saving). The mark-paid sheet now asks "Did this save you money?" and paid bills have "Not actually paid". Bear still never estimates a saving.
- **Less setup, lists and edits (25 Oct):** Non-bill captures Bear understands are set up automatically (area, project, steps and a reminder plan) and the person is told afterwards, with a "Bear set this up" note and an Edit button; bills always stay behind the verification check. Switch it off under Me. Tasks can be edited at any time, including completed ones (the Win follows a renamed title), and a completed task can be reopened, which removes its Win. Shopping and supply lists become checklists: tick items off over one trip or several, "Got everything", and the last tick finishes the task. Costs are estimated by the AI service per item (rough, labelled as estimates, never invented without the service) and a list can be split into 2 to 4 purchases balanced by cost and spaced before the due date. 174 tests, tsc, scoped lint, build and a 25-step walk-through pass.
- **Any task, not only bills (24 Oct):** Give it to Bear accepts text, typed detail, photo, screenshot, PDF and a **voice note** (on-device speech recognition, en-AU, editable transcript). Bear reads whatever it is given (school notes, appointment letters, forms, messages, receipts, bills) and classifies it as a bill or a general task with kind, area, when, who, where and steps. Bills keep the full payment workflow; everything else gets its own verify screen ("Have I got this right?"), task detail page, reminder plan, completion and win. PARA stays internal: every captured thing lands as a Project inside an Area with its Resource attached, and "Everything Bear is holding" under Me shows Areas → Projects → open steps, with Archive counted. Needs-you and home copy now cover all held things. 165 tests, tsc, lint, build and a 24-step walk-through pass.
- **P0 bill workflow, end to end and real:** sign-in → onboarding (Areas, People, PINS, Life Rhythm, Capacity) → Give it to Bear (text, typed, photo, screenshot, PDF) → Bear-thinking extraction (rule-based always; Anthropic vision when a key exists) with per-field confidence and provenance → "Have I got this right?" with specific questions for uncertain fields and user corrections recorded → Area/Project/Task/Bill creation with warranted dependencies → reminder plan from the capacity map and coordination engine → reminders actually scheduled (rows + in-app delivery + local notification + Web Push when configured) → notification decision engine (no guilt, quiet hours, day mode, deferral changes the plan) → assistance (payment page, BPAY, copy reference, original bill, explain, draft a question) → mark paid → Completion, Win, defensible Wealth entry, recurring expectation → My Wins, My Bear ledgers → Bear Ideas (recurring, discount, increase, related areas, missing expected; "Not relevant" trains suppression).
- Chaos Mode (How's today?), first-run example bill clearly labelled and excluded from totals, empty/loading/error/offline states, accessibility basics (labels, focus rings, reduced motion, large targets, no colour-only meaning).
- Supabase migration for 21 `bear_*` tables with owner-only RLS and a private `bear-documents` bucket; request-scoped Supabase store; in-memory store for tests and local development.
- Tests: 202 passing (vertical slice, edge cases, extractor, decision engine, capacity, coordination, store isolation). TypeScript, lint and production build clean. 27-step Chromium walk-through of the real screens passes (`docs/load-bear/07-verification.md`).
- Cron delivery route + Vercel cron (hourly) + GitHub Actions workflow (15-minute) for push reminders.
